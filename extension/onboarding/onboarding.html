FZER0

This plugin needs you to accept the use of the microphone. We are not recording or keeping any information about you or your conversations.

FZER0 can't run without the microphone

This extension has no purpose without microphone access. To remove it, go to chrome://extensions, find FZER0, and click Remove.

Before you join a call

  • Headset mandatory
  • Measures volume

Do you know your fundamental tone?

Find your fundamental tone first

This is not a medical device. If you have doubts, go to your speech pathologist to find out your fundamental tone to start using FZER0.

Set your range and target

Set your speaking level

Talk normally for five seconds — anything, out loud, at the volume you'd use on a call.

This sets the Target mark on the dial, and makes the reference tone play at your own speaking volume so you never push your voice to match it. You only do this once.

Chrome will ask for the microphone.

You're set up

Join any Google Meet call — the panel will appear on its own. Meet will ask for the microphone once, on its own page.

Anything here can be changed later from the ⋯ button on the panel, including your speaking level.
